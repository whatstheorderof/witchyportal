We pick up energy everywhere — at work, online, in crowded places. Energy cleansing is simply a way of clearing it and coming back to yourself.

## For you

- **Salt bath or shower:** add a handful of salt to your bath, or rub a little salt scrub over your arms in the shower, imagining the heaviness washing away.
- **Breath and sound:** take three deep breaths, sighing out loud on each exhale. Humming works too.
- **Earth:** stand barefoot on grass or sand for a few minutes.

## For your home

- **Fresh air:** open every window, even in winter, for ten minutes.
- **Sound:** ring a bell, clap or play music in each corner of each room.
- **Smoke (optionally):** herbs such as rosemary or lavender, used safely with a window open.
- **Declutter:** energy gets stuck where things pile up.

Finish by setting an intention for the space: *“Only love and peace may stay here.”*
