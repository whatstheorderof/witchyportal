We all dream every night, but most dreams vanish within minutes of waking. A dream journal catches them before they go.

## How to start

- Keep a notebook and pen beside your bed — not your phone.
- As soon as you wake, before moving too much, write whatever you remember: images, colours, people, feelings.
- Give the dream a title. It helps you remember it later.
- Note what's happening in your life right now — dreams often respond to it.

## Common symbols

- **Water:** emotions. Calm water, calm feelings; stormy seas, overwhelm.
- **Teeth falling out:** worries about how you're seen, or a change you feel unprepared for.
- **Being chased:** something you're avoiding in waking life.
- **Flying:** freedom, or a wish to rise above a situation.

Symbols are personal, though. What does the image mean *to you*? After a few weeks, your own dream language starts to appear.
