A tarot deck has 78 cards: **22 Major Arcana** (big life themes, like The Moon or The Star) and **56 Minor Arcana** in four suits — Cups (emotions), Wands (passion), Swords (thoughts) and Pentacles (money and the material world).

## Your first reading

1. Hold the deck, breathe, and think of a gentle, open question: *“What do I need to know today?”*
2. Shuffle until it feels right.
3. Pull one card and look at it before reaching for the guidebook. What do you notice first — colours, figures, feelings?
4. Then read the traditional meaning, and see how the two fit together.

## Tips

- Keep a tarot journal of your daily card.
- Reversed (upside-down) cards can be read as the energy being blocked or internal — or you can simply ignore reversals when starting out.
- There are no “bad” cards. Even Death usually means an ending that makes room for something new.
