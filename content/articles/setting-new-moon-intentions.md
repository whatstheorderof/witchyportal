The **new moon** is the start of the lunar cycle: the sky is dark, and anything is possible. It's the most powerful moment of the month to plant new intentions.

## How to do it

1. **Choose your moment.** The next new moon is on 10 October, in Libra. You can do your ritual that evening or in the two days after.
2. **Create a little ceremony.** A candle, a cup of tea, a quiet corner.
3. **Write your intentions in the present tense** — “I am calm and confident at work”, not “I want to be less stressed”.
4. **Keep them few.** Three to ten intentions is plenty.
5. **Take one action** towards one of them within 48 hours. Magic loves momentum.

## Libra new moon ideas

- I give myself the same kindness I give others
- My relationships are balanced and nourishing
- I create beauty in my home and my days

Come back to your list at the full moon on 26 October and notice what has begun to grow.
