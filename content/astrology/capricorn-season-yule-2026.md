The **winter solstice** falls on **21 December 2026**, when the Sun enters **Capricorn**. It's the longest night of the year, celebrated as **Yule**: from here, the days slowly grow longer.

Capricorn is patient, grounded and ambitious — the mountain goat that reaches the summit one steady step at a time.

## Key dates

- **21 December — Winter solstice / Yule.** Rest in the dark, then welcome the returning light.
- **24 December — Full Moon in Cancer.** A tender, homely moon — perfect for family, comfort food and emotional release.
- **26 December — Mercury enters Capricorn.** Plans become practical.
- **7 January — New Moon in Capricorn.** The most powerful moment to set your goals for 2027.

## A Yule ritual

On the solstice evening, sit in the dark for a few minutes and name what this year taught you. Then light a candle to welcome the returning Sun, and write three intentions for the year ahead. Keep the paper somewhere safe until the next solstice.
