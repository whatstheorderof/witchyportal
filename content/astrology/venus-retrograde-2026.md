Venus — the planet of love, beauty, pleasure and money — appears to move backwards from **3 October to 14 November 2026**, travelling from Scorpio back into Libra (it changes sign on 25 October).

Retrogrades aren't disasters. They're an invitation to **review, revisit and reflect** before moving forward.

## What it can bring up

- Exes or unfinished conversations resurfacing
- Questions about what you truly value — in love and in how you spend
- A desire to change your look (wait before anything drastic!)

## Working with it

- **Journal:** what did past relationships teach you about what you need?
- **Declutter:** clothes, beauty products, gifts that no longer feel like you
- **Hold off** on signing big contracts, major purchases or dramatic haircuts if you can
- **Rekindle** old hobbies and pleasures you've let slip

Venus turns direct on 14 November in Libra. Whatever you've reflected on, you'll be ready to act on it with clearer eyes.
