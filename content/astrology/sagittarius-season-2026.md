On **22 November** the Sun moves into **Sagittarius** — the archer, the explorer, the philosopher. After Scorpio's intensity, we get a breath of fresh air: optimism, curiosity and a hunger for meaning.

## Key dates

- **24 November — Full Moon in Gemini.** Conversations, ideas and connections come to a head. Say the thing you've been meaning to say.
- **26 November — Mars enters Virgo.** Energy turns practical: routines, health and getting organised.
- **6 December — Mercury enters Sagittarius.** Big-picture thinking and honest words.
- **9 December — New Moon in Sagittarius.** Set intentions for travel, learning and adventure.
- **11 December — Saturn turns direct in Aries**; **13 December — Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo.** Time to build steadily and reflect on how you shine.

## A Sagittarius ritual

Write a list of places — real or imagined — that your soul is calling you to. Pick one small step towards one of them and take it before the solstice.
