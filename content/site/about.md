Yulia Moon is a practising witch, spiritual guide and dream interpreter, and the winner of **Miss Generation 2025**. She hosts the **Yulia Moon Show** on YouTube — her cosmic corner for tarot readings, spellwork, astrology and all things mystical.

Each week she shares:

- Collective and themed tarot readings
- Moon rituals and spell tutorials
- Dream interpretations and energy cleansing
- Astrology forecasts and favourable date planning
- Magical product spotlights
- Spiritual Q&A — questions sent in by her community on Instagram

Her approach is grounded wisdom with a touch of glamour: helping you navigate love, life and the stars, and creating a safe space to learn, explore and reconnect with your inner power.
